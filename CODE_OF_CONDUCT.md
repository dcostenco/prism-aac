# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Prism AAC is built for children with complex communication needs and the families and clinicians who support them. We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone — particularly for AAC users, families, clinicians, and contributors with disabilities.

## Our Standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming, inclusive, and person-first language
- Respecting differing viewpoints, experiences, and clinical traditions
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Centering the needs of AAC users and their families in product decisions
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments — public or private
- Ableist language, including dismissing AAC users' communication as "less than" speech
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, code comments, commit messages, discussions, chat channels, and any public space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent to **`conduct@synalux.ai`**. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Project maintainers will follow these guidelines:

1. **Correction** — A private warning, with clarification of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — A public or private warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with those involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent Ban** — A permanent ban from any public interaction within the project.
