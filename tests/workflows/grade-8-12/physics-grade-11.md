# Physics — Grade 11

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF: every transformation on its own line, final answer with units.

Math panel categories used: `main`, `adv-math` (², √, x, t),
`physics` (m, s, kg, N, J, V, Ω, Hz, W, μ, λ, θ, Δ).

1. Acceleration — a car goes 0 to 60 mph in 6 s (60 mph ≈ 26.8 m/s).
   Find a.

   a = Δv ÷ Δt
   Δv = 26.8 − 0
   a = 26.8 ÷ 6
   a ≈ 4.47 m/s²

2. Newton's second law — net force on a 70 kg sprinter accelerating
   at 3 m/s².

   F = m × a
   F = 70 × 3
   F = 210 N

3. Kinetic energy — a 1500 kg car at 20 m/s.

   KE = ( 1 ÷ 2 ) × m × v²
   KE = 0.5 × 1500 × 20²
   KE = 0.5 × 1500 × 400
   KE = 300000 J
   KE = 3.0 × 10⁵ J

4. Ohm's law — 9 V battery across a 3 Ω resistor.

   V = I × R
   9 = I × 3
   I = 9 ÷ 3
   I = 3 A

5. Wave speed — a wave of frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 2 m.

   v = f × λ
   v = 50 × 2
   v = 100 m/s

6. Free-fall distance — object dropped from rest, t = 3 s, g = 9.8 m/s².

   d = ( 1 ÷ 2 ) × g × t²
   d = 0.5 × 9.8 × 3²
   d = 0.5 × 9.8 × 9
   d = 44.1 m

7. Momentum — 0.15 kg baseball at 40 m/s.

   p = m × v
   p = 0.15 × 40
   p = 6.0 kg·m/s
