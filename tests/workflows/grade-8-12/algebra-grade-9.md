# Algebra — Grade 9

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF `g.r.9_09_15_16_092016_0831PM.pdf`: every transformation on its
own line, final answer with units / labeled value.

Math panel categories used: `main` (digits + - × ÷ = .), `adv-math`
(x, y, a, b, ², √, ()), `misc-math` (≤ ≥ ≠).

1. Pizza shop — $12 base + $1.50 per topping. How many toppings can
   Maya buy with $21?

   12 + 1.50 t = 21
   1.50 t = 21 − 12
   1.50 t = 9
   t = 9 ÷ 1.50
   t = 6 toppings

2. Phone plan — $25/month + $0.10 per text. Carlos's bill was $33.
   Find the number of texts.

   25 + 0.10 x = 33
   0.10 x = 33 − 25
   0.10 x = 8
   x = 8 ÷ 0.10
   x = 80 texts

3. Two consecutive integers sum to 47. Find both.

   n + ( n + 1 ) = 47
   2 n + 1 = 47
   2 n = 46
   n = 23
   n + 1 = 24
   answer = 23 , 24

4. Ramp inequality — Ada must spend less than $50 on T-shirts at
   $8 each plus a $6 shipping fee. How many can she order?

   8 t + 6 < 50
   8 t < 50 − 6
   8 t < 44
   t < 44 ÷ 8
   t < 5.5
   t = 5 shirts

5. Distance-rate-time — a cyclist travels 45 km in 3 hours. Find the
   average speed.

   d = r × t
   45 = r × 3
   r = 45 ÷ 3
   r = 15 km/hr

6. Two-step word — Three less than twice a number is 17. Find x.

   2 x − 3 = 17
   2 x = 17 + 3
   2 x = 20
   x = 10

7. Area constraint — a rectangle's length is 4 more than its width
   and the area is 32. Find the width.

   l = w + 4
   l × w = 32
   ( w + 4 ) × w = 32
   w² + 4 w − 32 = 0
   ( w + 8 ) ( w − 4 ) = 0
   w = 4 ft
