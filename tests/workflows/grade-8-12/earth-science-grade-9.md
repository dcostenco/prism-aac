# Earth Science — Grade 9

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF: every transformation on its own line, final answer with units.

Math panel categories used: `main`, `adv-math` (², √), `earth-science`
(km, AU, ly, Mya, °C, °F, mph, mb), `misc-math` (×, ÷, ≈).

1. Earthquake S-wave / P-wave gap — P arrives at 10:00:00, S arrives
   at 10:01:30. Approximate distance (each 1 s gap ≈ 8 km).

   Δt = 90 s
   d ≈ Δt × 8
   d ≈ 90 × 8
   d ≈ 720 km

2. Plate tectonic motion — Atlantic plate spreads 2.5 cm/yr. How
   much in 1 million years?

   rate = 2.5 cm/yr
   t = 1 000 000 yr
   d = 2.5 × 1 000 000
   d = 2 500 000 cm
   d = 25 km

3. Temperature conversion — 25 °C to °F.

   °F = ( °C × 9 ÷ 5 ) + 32
   °F = ( 25 × 9 ÷ 5 ) + 32
   °F = ( 225 ÷ 5 ) + 32
   °F = 45 + 32
   °F = 77 °F

4. Distance to a star — Alpha Centauri is 4.37 ly away. Convert to
   km (1 ly ≈ 9.46 × 10¹² km).

   d = 4.37 × 9.46 × 10¹² km
   d ≈ 41.34 × 10¹² km
   d ≈ 4.134 × 10¹³ km

5. AU scale — Mars is 1.52 AU from the Sun. Convert to km
   (1 AU ≈ 1.50 × 10⁸ km).

   d = 1.52 × 1.50 × 10⁸
   d = 2.28 × 10⁸ km

6. Geologic age — fossil dated 65 Mya, dinosaur era ended at the
   K-Pg boundary. How many millions of years to today?

   start = 65 Mya
   end = 0 Mya
   age = 65 − 0
   age = 65 Mya

7. Pressure drop with altitude — surface 1013 mb, top of Mt. Everest
   ≈ 333 mb. Find Δ.

   Δ = 1013 − 333
   Δ = 680 mb
