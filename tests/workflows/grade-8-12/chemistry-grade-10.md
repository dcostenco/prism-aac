# Chemistry — Grade 10

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF: every transformation on its own line, final answer with units.

Math panel categories used: `main`, `adv-math` (², ()), `chemistry`
(elements H O C Na Cl Mg Ca Fe, →, ⇌, ₂ ₃ ₄, ⁺ ⁻, mol, (aq) (s) (l) (g),

1. Balance — sodium reacts with water.

   Na + H₂O → NaOH + H₂
   2 Na + 2 H₂O → 2 NaOH + H₂

2. Molar mass of NaCl (Na = 23, Cl = 35.5).

   M = Na + Cl
   M = 23 + 35.5
   M = 58.5 g/mol

3. Moles from grams — 117 g of NaCl, M = 58.5.

   n = m ÷ M
   n = 117 ÷ 58.5
   n = 2 mol

4. Combustion of methane — balance.

   CH₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O
   CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O

5. Stoichiometry — 4 mol CH₄ burned, how many mol H₂O produced?

   CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O
   ratio = 2 H₂O ÷ 1 CH₄
   H₂O = 4 × 2
   H₂O = 8 mol

6. pH calculation — [H⁺] = 1 × 10⁻³ M.

   pH = − log [ H⁺ ]
   pH = − log ( 1 × 10⁻³ )
   pH = − ( − 3 )
   pH = 3

7. Empirical formula — compound is 40 % Ca, 12 % C, 48 % O. Find
   the simplest ratio.

   Ca = 40 ÷ 40 = 1
   C  = 12 ÷ 12 = 1
   O  = 48 ÷ 16 = 3
   formula = CaCO₃
