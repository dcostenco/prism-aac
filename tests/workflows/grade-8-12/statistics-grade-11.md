# Statistics — Grade 11

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF: every transformation on its own line, final answer with units
or labeled value.

Math panel categories used: `main`, `adv-math` (², √), `statistics`
(μ, σ, σ², x̄, n, z, p, P(, H0, Ha, ≈, ≠), `misc-math` (∑).

1. Sample mean — quiz scores 7, 8, 6, 9, 10. Find x̄.

   ∑ x = 7 + 8 + 6 + 9 + 10
   ∑ x = 40
   n = 5
   x̄ = ∑ x ÷ n
   x̄ = 40 ÷ 5
   x̄ = 8.0

2. Z-score — exam score 85, μ = 70, σ = 10.

   z = ( x − μ ) ÷ σ
   z = ( 85 − 70 ) ÷ 10
   z = 15 ÷ 10
   z = 1.5

3. Probability of P(X > 1.5σ) for a standard normal distribution.

   z = 1.5
   P ( Z ≤ 1.5 ) ≈ 0.9332
   P ( Z > 1.5 ) = 1 − 0.9332
   P ( Z > 1.5 ) ≈ 0.0668

4. Sample variance — 4, 6, 8 with x̄ = 6.

   ( 4 − 6 )² = 4
   ( 6 − 6 )² = 0
   ( 8 − 6 )² = 4
   ∑ = 4 + 0 + 4 = 8
   s² = 8 ÷ ( n − 1 )
   s² = 8 ÷ 2
   s² = 4

5. Hypothesis test — sample p-value 0.03, α = 0.05.

   H0 : μ = μ₀
   Ha : μ ≠ μ₀
   p ≈ 0.03
   α = 0.05
   p < α
   reject H0

6. Confidence interval — x̄ = 50, σ = 4, n = 16, z* = 1.96 (95 %).

   SE = σ ÷ √ n
   SE = 4 ÷ √16
   SE = 4 ÷ 4
   SE = 1
   ME = z* × SE
   ME = 1.96 × 1
   ME = 1.96
   CI = ( 50 − 1.96 , 50 + 1.96 )
   CI = ( 48.04 , 51.96 )

7. Binomial — 5 coin flips, P(exactly 3 heads).

   n = 5
   k = 3
   p = 0.5
   C ( 5 , 3 ) = 10
   P = 10 × 0.5³ × 0.5²
   P = 10 × 0.125 × 0.25
   P = 0.3125
