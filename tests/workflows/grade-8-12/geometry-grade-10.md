# Geometry — Grade 10

Real-classroom word problems. Solution style mirrors the reference
PDF: every transformation on its own line, final answer with units.

Math panel categories used: `main`, `adv-math` (², √, π, x, r),
`geom` (°, △, ◯, A, P, V, r, d), `misc-math` (≅, ⊥, ∥).

1. Pythagorean theorem — a ladder leans against a wall, base 5 ft
   from the wall, top 12 ft up. Find the ladder length.

   a² + b² = c²
   5² + 12² = c²
   25 + 144 = c²
   c² = 169
   c = √169
   c = 13 ft

2. Circle area — pizza of radius 9 in. Find the area.

   A = π × r²
   A = π × 9²
   A = π × 81
   A ≈ 3.14 × 81
   A ≈ 254.34 in²

3. Triangle angle sum — two angles measure 47° and 68°. Find the
   third.

   a + b + c = 180°
   47 + 68 + c = 180
   c = 180 − 47 − 68
   c = 65°

4. Volume of a rectangular prism — box 6 × 4 × 3 cm.

   V = l × w × h
   V = 6 × 4 × 3
   V = 24 × 3
   V = 72 cm³

5. Similar triangles — △ABC ~ △DEF. AB = 6, DE = 9, BC = 8. Find EF.

   AB ÷ DE = BC ÷ EF
   6 ÷ 9 = 8 ÷ EF
   EF = 8 × 9 ÷ 6
   EF = 72 ÷ 6
   EF = 12 units

6. Surface area of a cube — edge 5 cm.

   SA = 6 × s²
   SA = 6 × 5²
   SA = 6 × 25
   SA = 150 cm²

7. Arc length — central angle 60° in a circle of radius 12.

   L = ( θ ÷ 360 ) × 2 × π × r
   L = ( 60 ÷ 360 ) × 2 × π × 12
   L = ( 1 ÷ 6 ) × 24 π
   L = 4 π
   L ≈ 12.57 cm
