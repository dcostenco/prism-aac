Prism AAC is the only free AAC app with on-device AI, Apple Watch, and 12 languages — built by a BCBA and parent to eliminate cost as a barrier to communication.

The Story

Why Prism AAC exists

More than 2 million Americans rely on augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), yet the leading AAC apps cost $249–$300. Research shows that 25% of speech-language pathologists cite cost as a primary barrier to AAC adoption. For many families, the price of a single app exceeds a month of groceries — forcing an impossible choice between communication and other basic needs.

Prism AAC is 100% free. Its AI runs entirely on-device, which means there are no cloud processing costs to pass along and no privacy risks from uploading sensitive communication data. The app works offline — critical in hospital WiFi dead zones, rural schools, and community settings where connectivity is unreliable. It also includes a standalone Apple Watch app, putting wrist-based communication within reach for users with mobility differences.

Dmitri Costenco is a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) and software engineer who built Prism AAC because he saw families unable to afford communication tools for their children. Drawing on years of clinical experience with individuals who use AAC, he designed an app that meets professional standards while removing every financial barrier. Prism AAC is developed by Synalux Health.
