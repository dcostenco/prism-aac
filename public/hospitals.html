The Communication Gap in Acute Care

When patients lose their voice, the gap between them and their care team becomes a safety risk.

👁

Patients Lose Their Voice

After intubation, stroke, TBI, or surgery, patients are awake and aware but unable to speak. Communication needs are immediate and urgent.

💰

Shared Devices Are Scarce

Hospital-owned AAC devices are expensive, shared across units, and difficult to sanitize between patients. Most floors have zero or one.

📶

WiFi Dead Zones

Many hospital areas have spotty or no WiFi. Cloud-dependent apps fail exactly when patients need them most.

📱

Family iPads Go Unused

Families bring personal iPads to the bedside, but without communication software installed, the device sits idle while the patient struggles.

Bedside Mode Features

Purpose-built for acute care, inpatient rehab, and post-surgical recovery. Download and hand to the patient.

📈

One-Tap Pain Scale (0–10)

Visual pain scale with numeric rating and faces. Patients tap to communicate pain level instantly. No training needed.

🔔

Nurse Call Button

Large, visible nurse call button that triggers an on-device alert. Works alongside existing call systems.

💬

Quick Phrase Cards

“I need water,” “I’m in pain,” “Call my family,” “I need the bathroom.” Pre-loaded phrases patients tap to speak immediately.

👉

Large Targets for Limited Mobility

Oversized, high-contrast buttons designed for patients with limited range of motion, tremor, or post-surgical weakness.

🎮

Switch Scanning + Eye Gaze

Compatible with switch scanning and eye gaze for patients who cannot use touch. Full access method support from day one.

⚡

No Login, No Setup

Download from the App Store and hand it to the patient. No account creation, no configuration, no onboarding wizard. Immediate use.

✈

Works WITHOUT WiFi — Fully Offline

Every feature runs entirely on-device. No internet connection required. Works in ICU, OR recovery, rural facilities, and anywhere WiFi is unreliable.

🔒

HIPAA-Safe: Zero Data Collection, Zero Cloud

No patient data is collected, stored, or transmitted. No analytics, no telemetry, no user tracking. All processing stays on the device. No BAA required.
