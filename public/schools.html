IDEA Compliance

How Prism AAC supports your district's obligations under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act.

What IDEA Requires

  • IDEA requires assistive technology consideration at every IEP meeting
  • Assistive technology must be provided at no cost to families
  • Schools must provide AT devices for home use if the IEP team determines it is necessary
  • AT must be available across all educational settings where the student participates

Free Tier & FAPE

Prism AAC's free tier provides a full-featured AAC solution at no cost, satisfying the Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) requirement for assistive technology under IDEA.

Premium & Clinical Tools

The premium tier adds clinician-facing analytics and reporting tools that help IEP teams track progress, document AT usage, and make data-driven decisions at annual reviews.

Home & School Use

Because Prism AAC is a free app on personal devices, students can use the same AAC system at home and school without district-owned hardware logistics.

No Barriers to Access

No account creation, no WiFi dependency, no recurring fees for the core product. Removes every barrier that typically delays AT provisioning.
